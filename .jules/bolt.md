## 2024-06-03 - Target Word Masking Optimization
**Learning:** When scanning large texts for many individual target words or phrases (e.g. in linting scripts), compiling a single alternation regex and using `finditer()` is much faster (O(L) complexity) than compiling and searching for each regex individually (O(N*L) complexity). Crucially, the target dictionary keys must be sorted by length descending (`sorted(keys, key=len, reverse=True)`) when building the regex to prevent shorter phrases from masking longer overlapping ones (alternation masking).
**Action:** When implementing linting scripts that check for many banned/warned words or phrases in large texts within the codebase, I should use a single pre-compiled alternation regex with `finditer()`, taking care to sort keys by length descending and maintain a mapping back to exact original dictionary keys to prevent KeyErrors.
