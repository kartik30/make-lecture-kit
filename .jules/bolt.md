## 2023-10-21 - O(L) Dictionary Checking with Combined Regex
**Learning:** When checking for many individual target words in large texts (like in linting scripts), using a single pre-compiled alternation regex `\b(word1|word2|...)\b` with `.finditer()` achieves O(L) time complexity per check, as opposed to iterating through the K dictionary keys and doing `.findall()` K times (which takes O(K * L) time). The combined regex approach is extremely fast. Note: sort keys by length descending `sorted(keys, key=len, reverse=True)` when building the regex to prevent shorter phrases from masking longer ones, and maintain a lowercase-to-original-key mapping to handle case insensitivity properly.
**Action:** Always prefer a single combined alternation regex for multiple string search/replacements over looping through targets individually when linting or scanning large documents.
